type: Changed

**The settings every program of gui_toolkit has**: zoom, density, rounding, animations, fonts and text rendering, scrolling, key and button repeats, tooltip and menu delays, and rendering, each on its page, the technical ones under a folded Advanced part. The look is now `appearance.theme`, `appearance.mode`, `appearance.finish`, `appearance.glow` (a level from 0 to 1) and `appearance.font_size`; the window's are `window.system_title`, `window.close_on_copy`, `window.width`, `window.height`, `window.shortcut` and `window.follow`. The `picker.*` names are gone: set again what you had changed.
